Shabbat Package 

Chabad of Bologna offers prepared kosher Shabbat food packages for visitors and local residents. Orders must be placed in advance and are subject to availability.

⏰
Order deadline Tuesday night by 22:00
📦
Pickup or delivery Available Friday after 16:00
🍴
Included Disposable cutlery is provided

Ordering information

Submit your order and we will contact you to confirm availability and pickup arrangements.

Your order is confirmed only after approval. If we are unable to fulfill it, you will be notified promptly and receive a full refund.

Kashrut

All food is prepared under the strict supervision of Rabbi Levi Braun and is certified Kosher Lamehadrin.
