Shabbat in Bologna

At Chabad

Friday night

Most weeks, we host a warm Shabbat dinner for locals and visitors. Kabbalat Shabbat takes place beforehand; a minyan is not guaranteed.

Please reserve by Tuesday night. For late requests or unavailable dates, contact us.

Reserve Shabbat dinner

At the synagogue

Shabbat day

Morning services are followed by lunch at the Jewish Community of Bologna. The synagogue entrance is at Via Mario Finzi 2.

For times, further information, and advance registration, please visit the Community's website.
