Chabad of Bologna

A Jewish home in Bologna

Welcoming Jewish students, local residents, families, and visitors from around the world.

Our story

Chabad of Bologna was established in 1976 by Rabbi Eliyahu and Mrs. Mindy Borenstein to create a welcoming Jewish home for students, residents, and visitors in this historic city and throughout the region.

In 2025, Rabbi Levi and Mrs. Menucha Braun moved to Bologna to continue this work and expand Chabad’s activities, hospitality, and community programs throughout the region.
