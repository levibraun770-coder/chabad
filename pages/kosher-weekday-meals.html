Kosher in Bologna

Gourmet Kosher Weekday Meals

Chabad of Bologna offers a gourmet kosher meal service for visitors and local residents.

Please note: This meal service is available for weekdays only. For Shabbat, you can order our specially prepared Shabbat Package.

Order Deadline

Weekday meal orders may be placed from two weeks up to three days in advance.

Pickup or Delivery

Pickup or delivery is available on weekdays after 18:30.

Included

Disposable cutlery is provided with your order.

Order Confirmation

Once your order has been reviewed, we will confirm whether we are able to fulfill it.

If we are unable to fulfill your order, you will be notified promptly and will receive a full refund.

Kosher Supervision

All food is prepared under the strict supervision of Rabbi Levi Braun and is certified Kosher Lamehadrin.
