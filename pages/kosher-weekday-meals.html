Kosher Meals in Bologna

Prepared kosher meals for weekdays and Shabbat. All orders must be placed in advance.

Ordering information

Orders must be placed at least 3 days in advance.

Disposable cutlery is included. All orders are subject to confirmation.

If an order cannot be fulfilled, you will be notified promptly and receive a full refund.

All food is prepared under the strict supervision of Rabbi Levi Braun and is certified Kosher Lamehadrin.
