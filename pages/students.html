Jewish Student Life in Bologna

New Jewish student in Bologna? Chabad offers meals, classes, events, holiday programs, and a welcoming community to help make Bologna feel like home.

Shabbat Dinner

Join us on Friday night for a warm, home-cooked Shabbat meal. Meet other students, enjoy great food, and experience Jewish community in Bologna.

Weekly Class

Relevant and engaging Torah classes designed for university students. No previous background is necessary.

Social Events

Connect through dinners, outings, activities, and informal gatherings with Jewish students from Bologna and around the world.

Holiday Programs

Celebrate Rosh Hashanah, Sukkot, Chanukah, Purim, Pesach, and other Jewish holidays with community, tradition, and meaningful programs.
