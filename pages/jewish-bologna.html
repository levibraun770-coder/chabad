About Jewish Bologna

Former Jewish Ghetto: Established in 1556 by papal decree between Via dell’Inferno, Via de’ Giudei, and Via Valdonica.

Synagogue: The present synagogue was built after the original was destroyed during the Second World War.

Jewish Museum: Documents Bologna’s Jewish history and culture across more than seven centuries.

Jewish Cemetery: The Jewish section of Certosa Cemetery remains in use today.
