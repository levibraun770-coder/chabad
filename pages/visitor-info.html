Practical information

The Chabad House is in the Cirenaica neighborhood, approximately a 10-minute walk from Porta San Vitale. Shabbat morning services are generally held at the historic Synagogue of Bologna, Via Mario Finzi 2.

For a mikvah appointment, contact Mrs. Menucha Braun at least three days in advance.
