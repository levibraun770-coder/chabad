About Chabad of Bologna

A home for Jewish life, connection, and community in the heart of Emilia-Romagna.

Our Story

Chabad of Bologna was established in 1976 by Rabbi Eliyahu and Mrs. Mindy Borenstein with the goal of providing a welcoming Jewish home for students, local residents, and visitors to this historic city and the surrounding region.

Since its founding, Chabad of Bologna has worked to meet the needs of the community through religious services, educational opportunities, social events, Shabbat meals, holiday celebrations, and a wide range of programs and activities.

In 2025, Rabbi Levi and Mrs. Menucha Braun moved to Bologna to continue this mission and expand Chabad’s activities throughout the region.

Chabad of Bologna is a place where everyone is welcome, regardless of affiliation or prior Jewish knowledge. Our purpose is to create a warm and inviting environment where people can explore and experience the spiritual inspiration and relevance of Judaism in a non-judgmental atmosphere.

Meet the Brauns

Rabbi Levi and Menucha Braun are the directors of Chabad of Bologna.

Rabbi Levi grew up in Sydney, Australia, and Brooklyn, New York. He pursued his studies in Jewish thought and practice in Tzfas, Israel, and at Yeshivas Oholei Torah in Brooklyn. After spending a year serving on Shlichus in Mexico City, he received rabbinical ordination from the Central Lubavitch Yeshiva at 770 Eastern Parkway. His infectious optimism and joyful personality drive his commitment to building a warm and welcoming Jewish community.

Menucha was born and raised in Aubervilliers, France. She is a passionate educator who teaches women and children. Her creativity, warmth, and attention to detail shine through the welcoming programs and events she plans at Chabad.

Together, they are dedicated to building a vibrant Jewish community in Bologna and creating meaningful Jewish experiences for every person who walks through the door.

About Chabad-Lubavitch

Chabad-Lubavitch is a philosophy, movement, and organization dedicated to strengthening Jewish identity, education, and connection. Guided by the teachings of the Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson, Chabad serves Jewish communities worldwide through thousands of emissaries in more than 100 countries.
